            Settings

            HumanWrite runs in your browser. Text analysis happens on this device; rewrites are sent straight from your browser to the Anthropic API using your own key.

            Create one at console.anthropic.com. It is sent only to api.anthropic.com.

            What HumanWrite does, and doesn't do

            • Rewrites for clarity, rhythm and natural flow, the way a good human editor would.
            • Keeps facts, statistics, names, dates, citations and your conclusion unchanged, and checks this after every rewrite.
            • Flags unsupported claims instead of inventing evidence.
            • Learns from your own writing samples when you ask it to.
            • Doesn't insert deliberate mistakes or swap words for random synonyms.
            • Doesn't claim to bypass Turnitin or any AI detector. That isn't its purpose.

            If you're a student, follow your school's rules on AI-assisted writing. Use HumanWrite to improve work you understand and can stand behind.
